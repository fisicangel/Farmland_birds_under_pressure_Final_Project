01 · The ecological signal02 / 09

Birds make landscape change visible.

Farmland birds respond to shifts in habitat, food availability and agricultural intensity. The index condenses many species’ population trends into one comparable signal.

An index of 70 means the monitored bird population is about 30% below its 2000 reference level—not that 70 birds remain.

02 · Research design03 / 09

How much of next year’s bird index can be predicted from its recent history—and do environmental indicators improve that prediction?

1

Collect

Eurostat birds, organic farming, pesticide sales, agricultural area, nitrogen and protected land.
2

Harmonize

Country-year keys, units, flags and pesticide intensity in kg/ha.
3

Engineer

Lagged bird indices, recent change, rolling information and environmental features.
4

Validate

Time-aware splits and cross-validation; future observations never train the past.

05 · Germany06 / 09
−45.3%

2000 to 2019

Germany’s Eurostat series falls from 100 to 54.72 over the common comparison period.

Why does the Eurostat line stop?

Germany did not stop monitoring birds. The post-2019 gap belongs to the harmonized Eurostat/OECD series. Germany later revised its national indicator and normalization, so newer national values cannot simply be appended to the 2000=100 series.

Analytical choice: retain post-2019 as missing—never zero, and never automatically extrapolated.

06 · Predictive modelling07 / 09

Complexity did not automatically win.

The strongest general forecasting model was Ridge regression on bird-history features. On the smaller matched environmental sample, Extra Trees gained a modest advantage when environmental variables were added.

08 · Conclusion09 / 09

Europe’s farmland-bird decline is broad but uneven. Recent bird history remains the strongest short-term predictor; environmental indicators add context and a modest predictive gain, but incomplete and methodologically changing series limit stronger claims.

What the model can do

Estimate next-year index values within the observed data regime.

What it cannot do

Prove that one agricultural pressure caused a national decline.

Next scientific step

Add climate, land-use and species-level abundance with explicit ecological lags.

Core message

Better biodiversity forecasting begins with better harmonized monitoring.
